KNPL-7 MEGA AUCTION

Auction rules at a glance

12 December 2026 · Chola Farm Resorts, Karukutty · The auctioneer's decision on any bid dispute is final.

Squad and points

  • 14 players per team, owner and drafted players included.
  • 1,00,000 points, one floating purse for KNPL-7, 8 and 9. Draft Points come off first; unspent points carry forward.
  • One login per team, used by one person, bidding with BID UP. A bid counts only when the platform records it and cannot be withdrawn.
  • If the platform or a connection fails, bidding pauses and resumes from the last recorded bid.

Skill Rating cap

  • The 14 players' Skill Ratings may total at most 560.
  • +25 bonus once in three seasons (squad cap 560 → 585, or retention cap 300 → 325). Declare it before the bid that needs it; final once declared.
  • Highest rating a team can buy = 560 − its current total − reserve for its other empty places.

Reserves on every bid

  • Points: keep the base point of the lowest category still available (2,000 while Rising Star Core players remain) for every place still empty after this player.
  • Skill Rating: keep the average rating of the Rising Star Core players still available, rounded up (about 19), for every empty place.
  • A bid is accepted only if it passes four checks: an empty place, the points reserve, the Skill Rating reserve and the category limits.

Extra place: opens once every Conqueror has been called (both rounds). A team that uses it can't buy a Conqueror later in Round 1; a team with a Conqueror can't use it.

Where they apply: 1st rounds of the Conqueror, Dominator and Rising Star Elite pools, and Rising Star Core players in the Rising Star Core pool. They are not squad limits: in 2nd rounds any team can buy, even a 2nd Conqueror or Apex.

Always: never more than 6 Conquerors + Dominators, so every team has at least 8 Rising Stars. Owner and drafted players count in all limits.

Calling order

Steps 1–2 · Conquerors1st round by Skill Rating, highest first, at the category Base Point, Round 1 limits. Straight after: 2nd round for the unsold at the next lower category's Base Point (CV 10,000 · CC 8,000), max 6 C+D only. Only if Conquerors remain after the draft.
Steps 3–4 · Dominators1st round by Skill Rating at Base Point, Round 1 limits. 2nd round for the unsold (DA 6,000 · DC 4,000), max 6 C+D only.
Steps 5–6 · Rising Star Elite1st round by Skill Rating at 4,000, Round 1 limits. 2nd round for the unsold at 2,000, no category limits.
Steps 7–8 · Rising Star Core poolRising Star Core players + every player still unsold, by random lot at 2,000. Round 1 limits for Rising Star Core players only. Then a 2nd round for the unsold. A Rising Star Core auction assistant is called at the end of Step 7.
Round 3 · Complete squadsOnly teams still short of 14; random lot at 2,000; max 6 C+D, cap and purse only. Short of points: release the player bought for the highest bid; short of rating room: the highest-rated. Never the owner or drafted players.
Round 4 · Cash bidOnly for released players. Closed cash bid, minimum the category Base Price; the winner takes him as a 15th player. The releasing team can't bid.

Same Skill Rating: order by a lot drawn before the auction (published). A group with fewer than 5 players joins the next lower group. Category and rating never change; only the opening bid drops.

Ties

  • When the current bid equals the highest bid another team is allowed, ask before closing whether that team also wants the player at the same amount. The first team to reach it does not win automatically.
  • If yes: closed cash bid by those teams only.
  • Minimum offer: the player's category Base Price. Highest offer wins at the tied points; equal offers: draw of lots.

After the auction

  • Unsold players form the reserve pool (not given to teams by lot).
  • Season replacement: only from the reserve pool, whole season, rating ≤ the player replaced; ₹500 paid by the replacement to the replaced player.
  • After KNPL-7 and KNPL-8: keep exactly 6 (owner included) within 300 Skill Rating and 84,000 points, buy 8 at the next auction.
  • Anything not covered: KNPL Core Committee decides; its decision is final.

LADDER ROUND 1 · EVERY TEAM

Rising Star Marque (RSM) match

Purpose: give Rising Stars a real chance to show their skill, and let teams test their bench strength.

8+

Playing 12

At least 8 Rising Stars (2 of them Rising Star Core). At most 4 Conquerors + Dominators, and only 1 Conqueror.

C

Captain

Must be a Rising Star.

1·2·3

Batting

Openers and No. 3 are Rising Stars. The Conqueror bats at No. 5 or lower.

✕

Retired out

No retired out in the RSM match.

6/10

Bowling

Rising Stars bowl at least 6 of the 10 overs, including the 1st and 10th.

IP

Impact Player

Must be a Rising Star (in normal matches: any category).

★

Rising Star of the Match

The top Rising Star in the match MVP list. If he is also Player of the Match, the award goes to the next Rising Star.

<8

Fewer than 8 available

All available Rising Stars play; the match referee decides the rest.

From the KNPL-7 Mega Auction Rules. Every Dynamic Ladder match must have a winner; a tie goes to a Super Over.
