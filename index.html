    Auction rules digest

    • Squad: 14 players per team, including the owner and drafted players.
    • Purse: 1,00,000 points per team — cannot be exceeded.
    • Skill Rating cap: sum of all 14 ratings ≤ 560, plus a one-time +25 bonus usable once (squad cap or retention cap) before the next mega auction.
    • Draft (optional): up to 3 incl. owner; only players who chose Madhura Mavericks as preferred team. "Any Team" players go straight to the pool. Each draft costs a fixed point deduction plus a mandatory draft fee (fee also acts as tie-breaker).
    • Draft point rounding: up to next 100 (1,000–2,000), 200 (2,000–8,000), 400 (8,000–10,000), 500 (above 10,000).
    • Category limits (proposed): once a team reaches its limit in a category it can't bid in that category in round 1; unsold players return in round 2 with no restriction. Confirm when rules publish on 25 Nov.
    • Platform: Super Player Auction Platform — one login per team, one bidder, BID UP. No second login with the same credentials.
    • Random pick: players come up by acrylic coaster draw.
    • Pool freeze: once the final pool (30 Nov) is published, no changes until next season.

    Tournament rules that shape the squad

    • KNPL-7: 11–14 Feb 2027, FISAT & Boundary Grounds, Angamaly. Daytime matches.
    • Format: 16 teams → Group A/B by lottery → 5 Dynamic Ladder rounds → top 4 to crossover QFs → SF → Final. Every ladder match needs a winner (Super Over).
    • Round 1 = RSM match: at least 8 Rising Stars in the playing 12; Conquerors allowed; no bonus point. A batter may retire out only after 6 balls.
    • Squad cycle: after KNPL-7, release 8 and retain 6. Retention cap (≈280 under consideration) includes the owner and is checked against revised ratings.
    • Word of Honour: commitment score (+5 / 0 / −5 per match) tracked across qualifying leagues — a signal of availability.

    Strategy playbook

    • The 40 rule. 560 ÷ 14 = 40 average. Each player rated 70 needs two or three low-rated Rising Stars to balance — price stars with that in mind.
    • Lock 8 Rising Stars. The RSM opener needs 8 in the XII. Don't let non-RS picks crowd out slots; the tracker warns when RS slots get tight.
    • Never spend the reserve. Max bid always keeps the minimum base for every empty slot. Beyond that number, a bid can strand the squad.
    • Watch rival headroom. If no rival can fit a player under their cap or purse, stop at base — nobody can push the price.
    • Auction pressure is real. Some high-rated players underperform in auction tournaments. Pay for rating, not reputation.
    • Think two seasons. The 6 retained after KNPL-7 must fit the retention cap on revised ratings. Young, improving Rising Stars are the best retention value.
    • Save the +25 bonus. Use it only if a must-have player won't fit — or keep it for retention.
    • Don't leave points unused. In Jan 2025, 2 teams finished under 80,000. Keep a late-round shortlist so the reserve turns into players. Round 2 (unsold, no category limit) can still yield value.

    Before auction day

    • Load the categorised pool with Skill Ratings (24 Nov) in Setup.
    • Update to the final pool list (30 Nov) — ratings will have the NPL-5 update and revised Awards.
    • Enter owner + drafted players for every team (draft lists confirmed 28 Nov).
    • Rank targets ★★★ / ★★ / ★ and set a walk-away price for each ★★★.
    • Check Super Player Auction Platform login and a backup device.
    • Keep the Skill Rating data confidential — committee and franchise owners only.

    1. Open script.google.com → New project, paste the code from apps-script/Code.gs in the mmwr repo.
    2. Project Settings → Script properties → add LOGIN_USER (your mobile number) and LOGIN_PASS (your password).
    3. Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Execute as Me, access Anyone → copy the URL.
