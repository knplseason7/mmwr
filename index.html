Rules used (Rule Book Rev. 3A)

  • 14 players incl. owner and drafts · 1,00,000 points · Skill Rating cap 560 (585 with the one-time +25).
  • Reserve on every bid: 2,000 points and the average Rising Star Core rating still available (~19) for each other empty place.
  • Round 1 limits (1st rounds only): top tier 4 = Conqueror 1 + Dominator 3 (Apex 1); a team with no Conqueror gets a 4th Dominator place once every Conqueror has been called · Rising Star 10 (Elite 7, Core 4). Always: max 6 Conqueror + Dominator.
  • Calling order: Conquerors → their 2nd round → Dominators → 2nd round → RS Elite → 2nd round (all by Skill Rating) → RS Core pool + all unsold (random lot, 2,000) → RSC 2nd round → Round 3.
  • 2nd round opens one category lower (CV 10,000 · CC 8,000 · DA 6,000 · DC 4,000 · RSE 2,000), no category limits.
  • Bid steps: +100 below 4,000 · +200 from 4,000 · +250 from 6,000 · +400 from 8,000 · +500 from 10,000.
  • Tie: the auctioneer asks before closing; closed cash bid decides.
